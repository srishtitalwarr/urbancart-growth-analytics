# Reflection

> **This is a demanding role that requires a lot of rigor and hard work. Why do you still want to apply for it?**

Because the demanding part is the part I enjoy.

This case study made that concrete for me. The most important finding wasn't a chart. It was 35 test accounts hidden inside the Affiliate channel. With them left in, Affiliate looked nearly as good as Google; once they were removed, it was the worst channel. One unchecked filter would have steered a ₹16 lakh budget decision in the wrong direction. I want to work somewhere that kind of care is expected rather than optional, because that is where analysis actually changes decisions.

I also like that this role doesn't stop at the answer. A CMO who asks the same questions every week needs a system she can trust, not another spreadsheet. Building the pipeline, the automated data checks and a question tool that refuses to guess is the end-to-end work I want to get better at, with data engineering, analytics and AI in one loop.

On AI: I used it heavily here and it made me much faster. It was also confidently wrong twice, and I only caught those mistakes because I checked the numbers myself. I think the people who do well in the next few years will be the ones who move fast with these tools and stay strict about what they produce. This role asks for both.

Hard work doesn't put me off when the problem is real and the feedback is honest, and a growth team that measures everything offers both. That's why I'm applying.
